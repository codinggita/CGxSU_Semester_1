6. What is the role of Next.js `instrumentation.ts`?

7. What is a request ID, and how can it help investigate a failed request?

9. What are Core Web Vitals? Briefly explain LCP, INP, and CLS.

10. What is a health-check endpoint? How is it different from monitoring application performance?
